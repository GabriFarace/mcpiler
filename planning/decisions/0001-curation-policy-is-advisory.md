# Curation policy recommendations are advisory

The MVP applies a deterministic curation policy to structural evidence and
validated semantic analysis, producing expose, hide, or requires-review
recommendations for a human. Deterministic evidence establishes hard blockers
and operational/action risk floors; model findings may preserve or increase
caution but may not lower those floors or override blockers, and missing,
invalid, or uncertain semantic output is handled conservatively. This preserves
meaningful semantic curation without treating probabilistic output as a
security control or automatically publishing generated tools. The fixed policy
exposes only sufficiently supported user-facing operations, hides only
sufficiently supported internal/infrastructure operations, and requires review
for high-risk or uncertain cases.
